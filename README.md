​1.1 Organisation Background 

​Bright Future Youth Tutoring Centre is an educational support organisation established to assist high school learners in improving their academic performance. The centre was founded to address a critical gap in local communities: the lack of accessible academic support for students outside of regular school hours. Many learners struggle with difficult subjects and fail to reach their potential simply because they do not have access to extra help, guidance, or resources. 

​The centre primarily serves learners aged 13 to 18, offering specialised tutoring in core subjects including Mathematics, English, Life Sciences, and Computer Studies. It provides a safe, supportive, and structured environment where students can ask questions, practice their work, and receive personalised guidance from qualified tutors. By focusing on both academic understanding and confidence building, the centre aims to help learners not only pass their subjects but excel in them. 

​1.2 Mission Statement 

​"To support students by providing quality tutoring and learning resources that help them succeed academically." 

​1.3 Vision Statement 

​"To create a future where every learner has access to the support and knowledge they need to reach their full potential." 

​1.4 Target Audience 

​High school learners between the ages of 13 and 18 (Grades 8 – 12) who need extra help with school subjects. 

​Learners who struggle with schoolwork or require additional reinforcement in difficult subjects. 

​Parents and guardians seeking reliable, safe, and effective educational support for their children. 

​Local schools and educators looking for external academic support partners. 

​Community members and stakeholders invested in youth development and education.

​2. Website Goals and Objectives 

​2.1 Motivation and Primary Goals 

​I chose Bright Future Youth Tutoring Centre because education is very important for the development of young people and communities. Many students struggle with their school subjects, especially Mathematics and Science, and they often do not have access to extra help. One of the biggest problems faced by small educational organisations is that people in the community may not know about the services they offer. Without proper promotion, many learners who need help may never find the tutoring centre. 

​Creating a website will solve this problem. The primary goals are: 

​Increase Visibility & Awareness: Ensure that when parents or students search online for tutoring services or academic help in the area, Bright Future Youth Tutoring Centre is easily found. 

​Provide Key Information: Share essential details such as subjects offered, tutoring schedules, contact details, and registration information clearly and easily. 

​Drive Enquiries & Registrations: Make it simple for parents and learners to express interest, ask questions, or sign up for programmes. 

​Establish Credibility: Present the organisation as a professional, reliable, and caring educational partner. 

​Improve Communication: Serve as a central hub for announcements regarding classes, holidays, or programme updates.

​3. Research and Comparative Analysis 

​To inform the website design and functionality, research was conducted on leading educational and tutoring platforms to identify best practices, features, and user expectations. 

​3.1 Competitor / Industry Analysis 

​Website 1: Khan Academy 

​Features: Free online lessons, educational videos, practice exercises, progress tracking. 

​Content: Comprehensive lessons and resources in Mathematics, Science, Computer Studies, and more. 

​Pages: Home, Courses, About, Resources, Contact. 

​Functionality: Highly user-friendly; visual and interactive learning tools; clear categorisation of subjects. 

​Key Takeaway: The website must present information in an organised, easy-to-navigate manner. Visual aids and clear explanations are essential. 

​Website 2: Tutor.com 

​Features: Online tutoring, live chat with tutors, student resources. 

​Content: Information about tutoring services, subjects offered, and learning support. 

​Pages: Home, About, Services, Resources, Contact. 

​Functionality: Students can connect with tutors online and receive help with homework. Focus on accessibility and immediate support. 

​Key Takeaway: Clearly explaining how the tutoring works and how to enquire/sign up is critical to building trust with parents and students. 

​Website 3: Varsity Tutors 

​Features: Online tutoring sessions, practice tests, learning tools. 

​Content: Includes tutoring services, test preparation, and academic support. 

​Pages: Home, About, Subjects, Resources, Contact. 

​Functionality: Allows users to find services and schedule sessions easily. Professional presentation with detailed service breakdown. 

​Key Takeaway: Detailed pages dedicated to specific subjects help users find exactly what they need quickly. 

​3.2 Summary of Requirements 

​Based on this research and the organisation's needs, the website must balance professional credibility with a welcoming, encouraging tone. It should prioritise clarity, ease of navigation, and accessibility of contact and service information. The focus is on converting visitors into enquiries, rather than acting as a repository of downloadable resources.

​4. Proposed Website Features and Functionality 

​4.1 Core Pages 

​Based on the requirements and research, the website will consist of the following key pages: 

​Home Page 

​Introduction and welcome message explaining who we are and who we help. 

​Overview of the centre’s purpose and key benefits. 

​Highlights of subjects offered (Mathematics, English, Life Sciences, Computer Studies). 

​Clear Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons: "Enquire Now" or "Contact Us". 

​Announcements section for upcoming classes or schedule changes. 

​Testimonials or success stories to build trust. 

​About Page 

​Detailed background of Bright Future Youth Tutoring Centre. 

​Presentation of the Mission and Vision statements. 

​Information on the approach to learning and the qualifications/experience of tutors. 

​Explanation of the problem being solved: lack of academic support and the solution provided. 

​Information on the safe learning environment provided. 

​Services / Subjects Page 

​Detailed list of subjects offered with descriptions of what support is provided for each: 

​Mathematics: Support for basic concepts, algebra, geometry, and exam preparation. 

​English: Reading, writing, grammar, comprehension, and literature support. 

​Life Sciences: Biology concepts, scientific terminology, and practical application. 

​Computer Studies: Basics of IT, software usage, and theoretical concepts. 

​Description of tutoring programmes (e.g., weekly sessions, exam preparation, homework support). 

​Information on schedules, times, and grade ranges supported. 

​Explanation of the learning methodology. 

​Enquiries Page (Replaces Resources focus) 

​Detailed information on how to register or book an assessment. 

​Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section addressing common concerns like pricing, session times, and class sizes. 

​Clear instructions on the next steps after making contact. 

​Contact Page 

​Physical address and location map integration. 

​Contact details: Phone number, Email address. 

​Detailed enquiry / registration form for visitors to submit their details and questions. 

​WhatsApp button for instant messaging support. 

​Operating hours. 

​4.2 Key Functionality 

​Mobile-First Responsive Design: Fully optimised for smartphones and tablets, as students and parents often browse on mobile devices. 

​SEO Optimisation: Structured data, meta tags, and keywords relevant to "Tutoring Durban", "Maths Help", "High School Tutors", etc., to improve search rankings. 

​WhatsApp Integration: Direct button allowing users to click and message the centre instantly. 

​Google Maps Integration: To help visitors easily find the physical location of the centre. 

​News / Announcements Module: Easy-to-update section on the homepage for schedule changes or holiday programmes. 

​User-Friendly CMS: Content Management System (WordPress) allowing staff to easily update text or add announcements without technical knowledge. 

​Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, ensuring the website is usable by all learners, including those with visual impairments. 

​Enquiry Management: Form submissions sent directly to email with clear fields for Name, Grade, Subject, and Message.

​5.4 Layout and Design Approach 

​Clear Hierarchy: Important information (Subjects, Contact) is always easy to find. 

​Card-Based Design: Used for listing subjects to create distinct, clickable sections. 

​Visual Icons: Simple icons representing subjects (e.g., Calculator for Maths, Book for English) to aid quick recognition. 

​Trust Signals: Quotes or testimonials from parents/students prominently displayed. 

​Call to Action: Buttons are distinct in colour (Yellow/Green) to stand out and encourage action. 

​Sticky Navigation: Easy access to menu items as users scroll.

​5.5 User Experience Considerations 

​Navigation: Simple menu structure ensuring users can reach any page within 2 clicks. 

​Readability: High contrast text, clear fonts, and adequate spacing – essential for an educational site. 

​Page Load Speed: Optimised images and code to ensure fast loading, even on slower mobile connections common in South Africa. 

​Forms: Simple but detailed enough enquiry forms to capture necessary information (Grade, Subject interest) to assist the centre in responding effectively. 

​Focus on Conversion: Every page leads the user towards making contact or sending an enquiry.
